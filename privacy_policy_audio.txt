Privacy Policy for Audio Recorder
Data Collection and Usage

The app requires microphone access to record audio. Files are stored locally on your device. The app uses Firebase Analytics (Google) to collect anonymous usage data such as app interactions and device identifiers to help improve the app.
Advertisements

The app uses third-party advertising services (Google AdMob). These services may collect certain data (e.g., device identifiers, approximate location from IP address) to display personalized ads. Please refer to Google's privacy policy for more information: https://policies.google.com/privacy
In-App Purchases

The app offers a one-time in-app purchase to remove advertisements. Payments are processed via the Google Play billing system. The app does not have access to your sensitive payment information.
Data Sharing

Your recorded audio files are never transmitted to any external servers.
Data Deletion Request

If you wish to request the deletion of any data associated with your use of this application, please contact us.
