Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This privacy policy is applicable to the Tchiki Mail app (hereinafter referred to as "Application") for mobile devices, which was developed by Jan Svoboda (hereinafter referred to as "Service Provider") as a Freemium service. This service is provided "AS IS".

Data Controller

Jan Svoboda (j7software) is the data controller responsible for this Application. Contact: j7software@post.cz.

Information Collection and Use

The Application processes the information you supply to connect your e-mail account(s) (e-mail address, credentials or OAuth tokens) solely to provide e-mail client functionality. This data is stored locally, encrypted, on your device and is not transmitted to the Service Provider's own servers.

The Application may collect certain technical information automatically — app version, device/OS type, country, and anonymized usage/session data — for analytics and app-stability purposes, subject to your consent as described below.

Data Encryption and Safety

All data transmitted by the Application is encrypted in transit (TLS) between your device and your e-mail provider's servers. Locally stored account credentials are encrypted using AES-256.

Third-Party Services

The Application integrates with the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Microsoft Identity Platform / Microsoft Graph API (Outlook / Office 365 account access)
  • Google Play Services
  • Google Play Billing (in-app subscriptions)
  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics
  • Google Play Integrity API (app/device integrity verification)

Analytics and Crash Reporting

On first launch, the Application asks for your consent before collecting any anonymous usage data (such as app sessions, device type, OS version and country) via Google Firebase Analytics. This data does not include e-mail content, credentials, or any personally identifiable information. You can review or change your choice at any time in the app: Settings → About → "Share anonymous usage data".

The Application uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to automatically report crash data in order to improve app stability. Crash reports contain technical diagnostic information about the device and app state at the time of the crash, but no personal data. Crash reporting is independent of the analytics consent above, based on the Service Provider's legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in maintaining a stable and secure app.

App Integrity Verification

To protect against tampered or repackaged copies of the Application (anti-fraud / license verification), the Application uses the Google Play Integrity API. A device integrity signal is generated on your device and verified server-side by the Service Provider's Cloud Function. This process does not involve any personal data; verification results are logged for a limited period for fraud-monitoring purposes only.

Data Retention

  • Locally stored account data (credentials, messages): kept until you remove the account or uninstall the App.
  • Analytics data: retained per Google Firebase's standard retention period, independent of the Service Provider.
  • Crash reports and integrity-check logs: retained for a limited period (typically up to 30 days) for debugging and fraud-monitoring purposes.

Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability where applicable. You can exercise these rights by contacting j7software@post.cz. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection supervisory authority (in the Czech Republic: Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, www.uoou.cz).

Account Deletion

Tchiki Mail does not operate its own user-account server — it connects to your existing e-mail provider. You can delete all data associated with the App at any time by removing your e-mail account in Settings, or by uninstalling the App. This immediately and permanently deletes all locally stored tokens, credentials, and messages from your device.

Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone below the applicable age of digital consent in their country of residence. If the Service Provider becomes aware that a child's personal data has been collected without appropriate consent, it will be deleted.

International Data Transfers

Some third-party providers listed above (Microsoft, Google) may process data on servers located outside the European Economic Area. Where this occurs, those providers rely on their own legally recognized data-transfer safeguards, as described in their respective privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact
