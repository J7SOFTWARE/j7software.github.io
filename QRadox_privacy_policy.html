2. Summary

QRadox is a QR code and barcode scanner. Scanning happens entirely on your device. We do not require you to create an account, we do not run our own servers that store your scans, and we do not sell your personal data. The App does use a small number of third-party services (described in Section 5) to show ads, process the optional "Remove Ads" purchase, and check for app updates - these are operated by Google and are subject to Google's own privacy policy.

3. Information We Collect and How We Use It

3.1 Camera

The App requests camera access to scan QR codes and barcodes. Camera frames are processed on your device (using Google's ML Kit and the ZXing library, both of which run locally) to detect and decode codes. Camera images are not uploaded to us, stored as photos, or transmitted anywhere by the App.

3.2 Photos You Choose to Scan

If you use the "scan from photo library" option, the App reads only the specific image you select through the system photo picker, decodes it on-device, and does not retain a copy of the image itself.

3.3 Scan History

When a code is scanned, its decoded content, detected format, timestamp, favorite status, and any note you add are saved in a local database on your device. This history:

  • is never transmitted to us or to any server we operate;
  • stays on your device until you delete individual entries, clear the app's data, or uninstall the App;
  • may be included in standard Android device backups if you have that feature enabled in your phone's own settings - this is a function of your device's operating system, not something the App sends anywhere on its own.

3.4 Contacts and Calendar

For certain scanned codes (such as a contact card or calendar event), the App can offer to open your device's Contacts or Calendar app with pre-filled details so you can save the entry yourself. The App does not read your existing contacts or calendar and does not save anything to them without you confirming the action inside that other app.

3.5 Location Data in Scanned Codes

If a scanned code contains geographic coordinates, the App can open them in a maps application. The App does not access your device's own location/GPS.

4. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require or offer account creation or login.
  • We do not access your device's precise location.
  • We do not sell personal data to third parties.

6. Consent & Advertising Choices

Your consent choice, collected via the Google User Messaging Platform described above, controls both ad personalization and whether Firebase Analytics collects data. You can review or change this choice at any time from within the App via Settings > Privacy Options. You can also manage ad personalization for your Google account at adssettings.google.com, and you can reset or opt out of the advertising identifier used for ads in your device's system settings. Purchasing the "Remove Ads" option within the App permanently removes ad banners from the App itself, but does not by itself change your Analytics consent choice.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your scan history is stored only on your device for as long as the App is installed, or until you delete entries yourself. Uninstalling the App removes this locally stored data (subject to your device's own backup settings, see Section 3.3). We do not retain any copy of it ourselves, as we do not receive it.

8. Your Rights (GDPR and Similar Laws)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction with similar data protection laws, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port information relating to you, and to object to certain processing. Because scan history is stored only locally on your own device and not on our servers, most of these rights are already directly in your control through the App's own History screen and your device settings. For questions about data processed by the third-party services in Section 5 (e.g. AdMob, Google Play Billing), please also refer to Google's own privacy tools linked above. You may also contact us at j7software@post.cz with any request, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

9. Children's Privacy

QRadox is not directed at children and is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required by your local law). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The App displays general-audience advertising via Google AdMob.

10. Security

Scan history is stored in a standard local application database, protected by your device's own operating system sandboxing. As with any software, no method of storage is 100% secure, but we do not transmit this data off your device, which limits exposure compared to cloud-stored alternatives.
