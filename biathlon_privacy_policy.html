Privacy Policy – Biathlon Rush: Mass Start

Effective date: 4 November 2026 (previous version: 29 October 2026)

This policy describes how the mobile game Biathlon Rush: Mass Start (“the game”), published by J7SOFTWARE (www.j7software.eu), handles information. In short: we (J7SOFTWARE) do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data. If you use Google Play Games, your race times and achievements are sent to Google (see below).

Information the game does not collect

  • No account or registration is required.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs for advertising or analytics.
  • We have no servers of our own – nothing is sent to us.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Data stored only on your device

Your settings (language, music, chosen country and category), personal best times, tournament progress, saved races and the information whether you have unlocked the full version are stored only in the game's private storage on your device. This data is never sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

The name you may type in for your racer is shown only in the game on your device.

In-app purchase

The full version is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. The game only asks Google Play whether the full version has been purchased, so it can unlock it. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play Games (online leaderboards and achievements)

The game uses Google Play Games Services, provided by Google, for online leaderboards and achievements. If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, the game sends to Google: your finishing times on medium difficulty (one leaderboard per course and event), and the achievements you unlock. Google shows your Play Games profile name, avatar and these results in the leaderboards and achievements. We can see only the same leaderboard and achievement data that other players see. This data is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Games settings on your device (where you can also change your profile visibility or stop using Play Games). If you are not signed in to Google Play Games, nothing is sent.

Children

We do not collect personal data from anyone, including children. Google Play Games has its own age requirements and settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: j7software@post.cz
