Privacy Policy – Biathlon Rush: Mass Start

Effective date: 29 October 2026

This policy describes how the mobile game Biathlon Rush: Mass Start (“the game”), published by J7 Software (www.j7software.eu), handles information. In short: the game does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data.

Information the game does not collect

  • No account or registration is required.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs for advertising or analytics.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Data stored only on your device

Your settings (language, music, chosen country and category), personal best times, tournament progress, saved races and the information whether you have unlocked the full version are stored only in the game's private storage on your device. This data is never sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

The name you may type in for your racer is shown only in the game on your device.

In-app purchase

The full version is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. The game only asks Google Play whether the full version has been purchased, so it can unlock it. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The game does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: j7software@post.cz

Co hra nesbírá

  • Není potřeba žádný účet ani registrace.

Jméno, které můžete zadat svému závodníkovi, se zobrazuje jen ve hře ve vašem zařízení.
