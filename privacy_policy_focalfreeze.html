Privacy Policy

FocalFreeze: Magnifier & Light — Android application by J7Software

Effective date: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data FocalFreeze: Magnifier & Light ("the App", "FocalFreeze") collects, how it is used, and who it may be shared with. The App is developed and published by J7Software ("we", "us", "our").

By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Summary

FocalFreeze is a camera-based magnifier and flashlight app that works entirely offline for its core function. We do not operate any backend server for the App, we do not require account creation or sign-in, and we do not collect, store, or transmit your camera images or frozen frames anywhere. The data leaving your device is limited to what is necessary for displaying ads (unless you purchase ad removal), processing an in-app purchase, and basic app analytics/crash reporting — all handled by Google's Firebase and AdMob services, not by any server of our own.

2. Camera access

The App requests the Camera permission because it is the App's core function — turning your device into a magnifier. Specifically:

  • The live camera preview is displayed on screen and is never recorded, saved, or transmitted anywhere.
  • The "Freeze" feature captures a single frame and holds it in your device's memory for viewing (with pinch-zoom and pan) until you unfreeze or close the app. This frame is never written to your photo gallery, never uploaded, and is discarded from memory once you unfreeze the image or leave the app.
  • We do not use the camera for any purpose other than displaying the magnified/frozen image to you, on your own device.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App displays banner advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and process certain data to serve and measure ads, which can include:

  • Advertising identifiers (such as your Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, language, screen size)
  • App interaction data related to ads (impressions, clicks)

Before ads are loaded, the App displays a consent form through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) where required by law (e.g. for users in the European Economic Area and the UK), allowing you to manage your consent for personalized advertising. If consent is required and not given, or if loading fails for any reason, the App continues to function normally without ads.

This processing is governed by Google's own privacy policy, not by us directly:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google's Ad Partner Policy

4. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The App offers a one-time, optional in-app purchase ("Remove Ads") that permanently disables ads. This purchase is processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We do not receive, see, or store your payment details (card numbers, billing address, etc.) — this information is handled directly by Google in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy. We only receive confirmation from Google Play that the purchase was completed, so the App can unlock the ad-free state on your device (and restore it automatically if you reinstall the App or sign in on a new device with the same Google account).

5. Analytics and crash reporting (Firebase)

The App uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, services provided by Google, to help us understand how the App is used and to detect and fix crashes or technical errors. These services may collect:

  • App usage data, such as which screens or features are opened and how often the App is used (Analytics)
  • Device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, language, country/region derived from IP address)
  • Installation and instance identifiers (such as the Firebase Installation ID and Google Advertising ID)
  • Crash and error reports, including stack traces, at the moment the App encounters a technical problem (Crashlytics)

This data is used solely to understand app usage patterns and to identify and fix bugs and crashes — it is not linked to your name, email address, or any account, since the App does not have accounts. We do not access your camera images or frozen frames through these services; they only report technical and usage information about the App itself.

For users in regions where the consent form described in Section 3 is shown, Firebase Analytics data collection is restricted by default (Google Consent Mode) unless our records show that consent is not required for your region. Crash reporting (Crashlytics) is not affected by this restriction, as it is necessary for us to keep the App stable and working correctly.

This processing is governed by Google's own privacy and data-processing terms, not by us directly:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase and Data Privacy

7. Data we do not collect

  • We do not require or ask for your name, email address, or any account registration.
  • We do not access your photo gallery, contacts, location, microphone, or files.
  • We do not operate any server that stores your camera images, usage history, or personal data.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone.
In short: any data collection described in this policy comes from Google's advertising, billing, and Firebase analytics/crash-reporting services embedded in the App (sections 3–5) — not from us directly. If you purchase ad removal, the advertising SDK is not even initialized, and no ad-related data is collected from that point on; analytics and crash reporting continue independently, as they help us keep the App working correctly for everyone.

8. Data retention

Since we do not operate a server or database of our own, we do not retain any personal data. Any data processed for ads, purchases, analytics, or crash reports is retained according to Google's own retention practices, described in the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase data privacy page linked above.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

10. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data on our own servers, there is generally no data held by us to access, correct, or delete. For data processed by Google (advertising identifiers, purchase records, analytics, and crash reports), you can manage your preferences directly through your Google Account settings, including resetting or opting out of ad personalization on your device (Settings → Privacy → Ads, on most Android devices). If you have questions about your rights under applicable law (such as the GDPR), you are welcome to contact us as well.
