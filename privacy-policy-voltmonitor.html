2. What Data the App Collects

Volt Monitor is designed around a principle of data honesty: it only displays values it can actually read from your device, and it stores as little as possible.

2.1 Battery data (read directly from your device)

The app reads the following values from Android’s system battery APIs whenever your battery status changes:

  • Battery percentage
  • Battery voltage
  • Battery current
  • Battery temperature
  • Charging status and plug type (USB / AC / wireless)
  • System-reported battery health
  • Remaining battery capacity (charge counter)

This data is used only to display live battery information, calculate charging/discharging estimates, detect charging sessions, and (optionally) show historical charts.

2.2 Optional background history

If you enable “Record in background” in the History screen, the app stores battery samples locally on your device in a local database (Room/SQLite). This data:

  • Never leaves your device. It is not uploaded to us or to any server we control.
  • Is automatically deleted after a retention period (30 days for the free version, 180 days if you purchase Volt Monitor Pro).
  • Is deleted immediately if you uninstall the app.
  • Can be disabled at any time via the same toggle.

2.3 Device diagnostics

The Settings screen shows technical information about your device (model, manufacturer, Android version, screen resolution, app version) for your own reference. This information is read live and displayed only within the app — it is not transmitted anywhere by us.

2.4 Data we deliberately do NOT collect or guess

Consistent with our data-honesty principle, the app never invents or estimates values it cannot measure, including:

  • Charging cycle count
  • Battery manufacturing date
  • Manufacturer-rated (“declared”) battery capacity beyond what a device optionally exposes via a non-public system API (shown clearly labeled as such, or as “Unavailable”)
  • The actual negotiated charger voltage (e.g. USB-PD/Quick Charge input voltage)

2.5 Information we do not access at all

Volt Monitor does not request or access your location, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera, or any other sensitive personal data.

2.6 Backup and restore (Pro feature)

Volt Monitor Pro lets you export your locally stored history and measured capacity data to a single file, and import it back later (for example, after reinstalling the app or moving to a new phone). This feature:

  • Is entirely user-initiated - the app never creates or uploads a backup on its own, in the background, or without you tapping the export button.
  • Uses Android's built-in file picker (Storage Access Framework) to save and open the backup file, so the app does not require or request any storage permission.
  • Saves the file only to the location you choose in that picker (e.g. your device's Downloads folder, or a cloud storage app you have installed) - we do not receive a copy, and the app itself does not transmit the file anywhere.

3. Third-Party Services

To keep the app free, and to offer an optional paid upgrade, Volt Monitor integrates the following third-party services. These services operate under their own privacy policies and may independently collect and process certain technical data (such as advertising identifiers) in accordance with their own terms:

3.1 Google AdMob (advertising)

The free version of the app displays “App Open” ads provided by Google AdMob when you return to the app from the background. AdMob may collect device identifiers and other technical data to serve and measure ads. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Ads are not shown:

  • If you have purchased Volt Monitor Pro.
  • During your first few app launches.
  • More than once within a short time window (frequency capping).
  • Before you have provided the required privacy consent (see Section 4).

3.2 Google Play Billing (in-app purchase)

If you choose to purchase “Volt Monitor Pro” (a one-time, non-consumable purchase), the transaction is processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Purchase status is verified directly with Google Play; a small local cache is kept only so the app can start up quickly and show your correct purchase status.

3.3 Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

We use Google’s UMP SDK to obtain your consent for personalized/non-personalized advertising where required by law (see Section 4).

Each of the above services is operated by Google LLC and is subject to Google’s own Privacy Policy.

4. Your Consent Choices (GDPR / UK / Switzerland)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app will show a consent form (via Google’s UMP SDK) before requesting any ads, asking for your consent to the use of cookies/local storage and personal data for advertising purposes, in line with the GDPR and the EU ePrivacy Directive.

You can review or change your consent choices at any time from within the app: Settings → Permissions & Privacy → Manage ad consent.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Live battery readings exist only in memory while the app is open and are not persisted unless background logging is on.
  • Locally logged history is deleted automatically after 30 days (free) or 180 days (Pro), and immediately upon uninstalling the app.
  • Since we do not operate our own servers that store your personal data, there is no remote copy of your data for us to delete on request — uninstalling the app (or disabling background logging and waiting for the retention period to pass) removes it completely from your device.
  • If you export a backup file (see Section 2.6), that file is no longer subject to the app's automatic retention period once saved - it stays wherever you saved it until you delete it yourself.
